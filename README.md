One original line (96 characters) roasting a gadget I use every day — my
wireless earbuds:

> My earbuds conspired against me — one dies at 40%, the other at 4%; they
> couldn't agree on when.

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes that
line to `/output/entry.txt`.
